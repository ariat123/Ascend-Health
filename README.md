# Ascend Health

Early product research for Ascend Health, an AI health-optimization app concept: personalized protocols, daily check-ins, progress tracking and weekly AI insights.

- [`files/market-research-health-optimization-app.md`](files/market-research-health-optimization-app.md): demand signals, competitors, target audience, user complaints about current tools, distribution, risks and next steps to validate.
- [`files/health-optimization-app-blueprint.md`](files/health-optimization-app-blueprint.md): positioning, the gamified structure, the AI layer, pricing and build scope.

Content guidelines for the app's social channels are in [ascend-health-content](https://github.com/ariat123/ascend-health-content).
